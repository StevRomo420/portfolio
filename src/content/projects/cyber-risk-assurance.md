I developed a practical risk workflow that connects technical review with business decisions. I identified relevant scenarios, assessed likelihood and impact, documented control gaps and maintained follow-up on agreed treatment actions.

I also planned controlled security-awareness exercises and translated their results into training priorities. The aim was not simply to produce reports, but to give each risk an owner, a treatment decision and a visible path to closure.

This public case describes the working method only. It excludes organizational identifiers, risk scores, findings, affected assets, responsible parties, timelines and internal evidence.
